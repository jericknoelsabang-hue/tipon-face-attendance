Security & limitations

Role checks are enforced by the server. Approved accounts have a password-encrypted offline lease. This is a controlled organization pilot: camera liveness, hardware attestation and tamper-proof GPS are not included. Secure the devices and complete a privacy/security review before payroll use.

Use an approved member privacy notice and retention policy. Photos are not stored; encrypted numerical face templates synchronize to authorized terminals. Do not treat face/GPS results as independently verified payroll evidence.
